Campaign structure

Three campaigns, one objective: booked consultations.

Lead objective on Meta with the booking page as the conversion event. Instant forms only as a backup for the OFW campaign where page load on mobile data is slower.

Campaign 01 · 55% of budget

Cold: "Boring business"

Advantage+ audiences seeded with business-owner, franchise and investing interests. NCR, Calabarzon, Central Luzon, Cebu, Davao. Ages 28–55.

  • Optimise for Schedule (booking) event
  • Dynamic creative: 3 angles × 2 formats
  • Daily cap ₱1,400 at the ₱2.5K tier
Campaign 02 · 25% of budget

Retargeting: "See the math"

Video viewers 50%+, funnel visitors 30 days, calculator users, form abandoners. Hits them with the ₱100K ledger and owner stories.

  • Frequency cap 3 per 7 days
  • Calculator screen-record creative
  • Objection ads: "no experience", "I'm abroad"
Campaign 03 · 20% of budget

OFW: "Business back home"

Filipinos living in UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan. Language Filipino + English. Lookalike of PH leads.

  • Messenger + instant form backup
  • Remote-owner angle, family runs the floor
  • Consultation via video call

TikTok + Reels organic engine

Three hook videos a week from the shop floor: coin counts, machine installs, "what ₱384K of laundry looks like." Best performers get ₱300/day Spark Ads.

Google Search safety net

Exact-match on "laundry business philippines", "laundromat franchise", "LG commercial washer price", "paano magsimula ng laundry shop". ₱300/day, straight to the booking page.

Launch creatives

Six ads, three angles: fear, math, freedom.

Mockups in Facebook feed format. Images are stand-ins for shop-floor photos and the owner portraits we'll shoot at install day.

Video hooks

Nine seconds to earn the next nine.

Hook 01 · The coin count

TikTok / Reels
  1. 0–2sClose-up: a tray of ₱20 coins poured onto a counter. On-screen: "One Tuesday. One shop."
  2. 2–6sOwner (VO): "Walang trend dito. Labada lang. Pero ito ang Tuesday ko."
  3. 6–11sCut to washers spinning, queue outside, water bottles lined up. Text: "₱12,800 today."
  4. 11–15sCTA card: "₱100K-a-month math. Link in bio. Free consultation."

Hook 02 · Trend vs need

TikTok / Reels
  1. 0–2sSplit screen: shuttered milk-tea stall / busy laundry shop. Text: "2023 vs 2026."
  2. 2–7sVO: "Every year there's a new 'sure-win' business. Every year people still have dirty clothes."
  3. 7–12sCalculator screen recording: sliders moving, profit line hitting ₱100,000.
  4. 12–15sCTA: "Build yours with us. Free 30-min call."

Hook 03 · Install day

Reels / YouTube Shorts
  1. 0–3sTruck doors open on LG machines. Text: "Day 1 of her ₱100K shop."
  2. 3–9sTimelapse: unboxing, plumbing, first test cycle. Owner smiling, thumbs up.
  3. 9–13sVO: "Location checked. Installed. Trained. Opening Saturday."
  4. 13–15sCTA: "Your install day is 45 days away. Book the call."
