Our story

We started selling bikes out of our own house.

August 2020 → todayConcepcion Dos, Marikina
AUG 2020

Lockdown. Jeepneys off the road. Families needed a way to move.

So we started an online bike shop from home. Orders kept coming, and the sala filled with boxes.

LATE 2020

The house became the bodega.

Frames in the garage, test rides on the street outside, and the first wholesale buyers lining up at the gate.

MAY 2021

3 Lilac St., Concepcion Dos.

The first real bodega opens. The promise never changed: pinakamaasahan at pinakamurang bikes & e-bikes.

TODAY

Wholesale, retail and resell, a second branch in Goodrich Village, and an e-bike battery warehouse so no customer is ever stuck with a dead unit.

Questions before you visit

Straight answers.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Frames and e-bike motors carry a shop warranty, and we do a free tune-up within the first 30 days. Bring the unit back to either branch. We fix it, no runaround.

Can I really test-ride before paying?

Always. Reserve online, the unit is set aside for 48 hours, and you ride it around Lilac Street before deciding. No deposit required to reserve.

Where do you deliver for free?

Anywhere in Metro Manila, free, usually next day. Rizal and nearby provinces are available at a flat rate. Ask us on Messenger with your barangay.

How does installment work?

A Home Credit agent is on site at the bodega. Bring two valid IDs; approval usually takes 15–20 minutes. Credit cards are also accepted for straight or installment payment.

I'm a reseller. How do I get wholesale pricing?

Tick "Reseller bulk" on the reserve form or message us. We'll send the current wholesale pricelist and stock sheet. Minimum order quantities apply per model.

What happens when my e-bike battery dies?

We keep a dedicated e-bike battery warehouse. Replacement batteries for the models we sell are in stock, and we install them for you. No 3-week wait for a shipment.
