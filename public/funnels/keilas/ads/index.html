01
The ads have one job

Get them to reserve a bike and book a free test ride.

We don't try to sell the bike inside the ad. We ask for something easy: hold a unit for 48 hours, come try it on Lilac Street. Once they're on the saddle, your staff makes the sale.

₱0to reserve. No deposit, so saying yes is easy.
48 hrswe hold the bike at the counter. Makes them come sooner.
About 1 in 5people who book a test ride end up buying. That's our target.
02
How it works

Four steps from scrolling to riding.

1They see the adOn Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. "Same bike, ₱4,000 cheaper than the mall."
2They tap itIt opens the funnel page with real prices, the bodega photos and the Home Credit option.
3They reserve + book a test rideThey type their name and number. Your staff gets a text right away and texts them back within the hour.
4They come, ride, buyThe bike is tuned and waiting. They try it. Most people who ride it, buy it.

07
If they don't book right away

Most people need to see you 3 or 4 times first. So we follow up.

7 days of reminders
Day 1They looked, but didn't bookThey visited the page, started the form, or watched half a video. Facebook remembers them.
Days 1–2"Naka-reserve na yung unit mo."A reminder that the bike is tuned, checked and waiting at the counter.
Days 3–5Answers to their worriesInstallment approved in 20 minutes. Free delivery. Warranty and batteries in stock.
Days 6–7Last reminder + MessengerA direct line to your staff with the stock sheet. After day 7 we stop, so it never feels like spam.

People who already bought, and your current customers, never see these reminder ads.

08
The simple math

Out of every 10,000 people who see the ad…

See the ad10,000Costs about ₱1,200 in ads
Tap it200About 20 out of every 1,000
Book a test ride20About 10 out of every 100 who tap
Show up to ride11A bit more than half who booked
Buy a bike3 to 4About 18 out of every 100 who booked. At ₱9,500 a bike, that's about ₱34,000 in sales.

So about ₱1,200 in ads brings in 3 to 4 bikes sold, about ₱34,000 in sales. These are average results for Facebook ads in the Philippines. Your real numbers will be higher or lower, and you'll see the real ones every week.
