Ads plan · Keila's Bike Shop Marikina Bodega · November 2026

Fill the bodega with riders who already want a bike.

Three Meta campaigns and one TikTok hook engine, all pointed at a single action: reserve a bike and book a free test ride. Every peso is measured by leads that walk into Lilac Street.

01
The one action

Every ad ends at the same door: reserve + free test ride.

A bodega wins when people walk in. So we don't sell bikes in the ad — we sell the 48-hour hold and the ride around Lilac Street. The sale happens on the saddle.

₱0deposit to reserve — the lowest-friction ask in Marikina
48hunit held at the counter, which creates urgency without pressure
1 in 5test rides we plan to convert into a bike out the door (sample target)
02
Campaign structure

3 Meta campaigns + 1 TikTok engine

Objective: leads → test rides
40%Campaign 01 · Retail

Marikina & neighbors

Families, first-time buyers, weekend riders within 12 km of the bodega.

  • Marikina, Pasig, QC, Antipolo, Cainta
  • Leads: instant form + funnel
  • Price-anchor + test-ride creative
35%Campaign 02 · E-bike intent

Commuters & seniors

People paying daily fares, PWD/senior mobility buyers, 3–4 wheel shoppers.

  • Interests: e-bike, Home Credit, commuting
  • Leads + Messenger
  • Fare-savings math, installment
15%Campaign 03 · Reseller

Wholesale lead gen

Sari-sari owners, bike resellers, online sellers in Luzon looking for supply.

  • Luzon-wide, 25–55, business owners
  • Messenger conversations
  • Bodega walkthrough, stock sheet
10%Engine · TikTok

Hook videos

Organic + Spark Ads on the shop's own TikTok. The bodega itself is the content.

  • 3 hooks/week, 15–30s vertical
  • Boost the top hook
  • Retarget viewers on Meta

Retargeting layer

06
Short-video hooks

Three scripts the staff can shoot on a phone.

15–30s · vertical · TikTok, Reels, Stories
Hook A · "Mall vs. Bodega"
  1. 0–3sMall receipt next to our price tag. "₱9,800 sa mall. ₱5,400 dito. Same bike."
  2. 3–10sWalk the aisle: MTBs, kids bikes, e-bikes. Price tags visible.
  3. 10–20sCustomer sits, rides out the door. "Test-ride muna. Bayad kung gusto mo."
  4. 20–30s"Libre delivery Metro Manila." CTA: reserve, link in bio, walang deposit.
Hook B · "Pamasahe math"
  1. 0–3sCalculator: "₱100 × 30 = ₱3,000." Voice: "Ganito ang pamasahe mo."
  2. 3–10sCut to e-bike. "₱0 down, Home Credit, 20 minutes sa store."
  3. 10–20sCommuter POV through Marikina streets at dawn.
  4. 20–30s"Bayad sa sarili in under a year." CTA: book a free test ride.
Hook C · "Bodega tour"
  1. 0–3sDoor opens on 200+ bikes. "Pinakamalaking bike bodega sa Marikina."
  2. 3–12sKids corner, MTB wall, e-bike row, battery shelf. "Batteries? Meron."
  3. 12–20sReseller loading 10 units into a van. "Wholesale? Meron din."
  4. 20–30s"Started sa sala namin, 2020." CTA: 3 Lilac St., open daily 9–7.
