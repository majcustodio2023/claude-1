Ads plan · Keila's Bike Shop Marikina Bodega

Fill the bodega with riders who already want a bike.

Three Meta campaigns and one TikTok hook engine, all pointed at a single action: reserve a bike and book a free test ride. Every peso is measured by leads that walk into Lilac Street.

① Funnel ② Ads plan ③ Projections
Campaign structure

3 Meta campaigns + 1 TikTok engine

Objective: Leads → Test rides
CAMPAIGN 01 · RETAIL

Marikina & neighbors

Families, first-time buyers, weekend riders within 12 km of the bodega.

  • Radius: Marikina, Pasig, QC, Antipolo, Cainta
  • Objective: Leads (instant form + funnel)
  • Creative: price-anchor + test-ride
  • 40% of budget
CAMPAIGN 02 · E-BIKE INTENT

Commuters & seniors

People paying daily fares, PWD/senior mobility buyers, 3–4 wheel shoppers.

  • Interests: e-bike, Home Credit, commuting
  • Objective: Leads + Messenger
  • Creative: fare-savings math, installment
  • 35% of budget
CAMPAIGN 03 · RESELLER

Wholesale lead gen

Sari-sari owners, bike resellers, online sellers in Luzon looking for supply.

  • Luzon-wide, 25–55, business owners
  • Objective: Messenger conversations
  • Creative: bodega walkthrough, stock sheet
  • 15% of budget
ENGINE · TIKTOK

Hook videos

Organic + Spark Ads on the shop's own TikTok. The bodega itself is the content.

  • 3 hooks/week, 15–30s vertical
  • Boost top hook with Spark Ads
  • Retarget viewers on Meta
  • 10% of budget

Retargeting layer across all campaigns: anyone who visited the funnel, opened an instant form, or watched 50% of a video gets the "unit reserved for you" ad for 7 days.

Short-video hooks

3 scripts for TikTok, Reels & Stories

15–30s · vertical · phone-shot
Hook A · "Mall vs. Bodega"
  1. 0–3sHold a mall receipt next to our price tag. "₱9,800 sa mall. ₱5,400 dito. Same bike."
  2. 3–10sWalk the aisle: rows of MTBs, kids bikes, e-bikes. Fast cuts, price tags visible.
  3. 10–20sCustomer sits on a bike, rides out the door. "Test-ride muna. Bayad kung gusto mo."
  4. 20–25sText overlay: "Libre delivery Metro Manila." Delivery truck loading.
  5. 25–30sCTA: "Reserve mo na — link sa bio. Walang deposit."
Hook B · "Pamasahe math"
  1. 0–3sCalculator on screen: "₱100 × 30 days = ₱3,000." Voice: "Ganito ang pamasahe mo."
  2. 3–10sCut to e-bike. "₱0 down, Home Credit, 20 minutes sa store."
  3. 10–20sCommuter POV riding through Marikina streets, dawn light.
  4. 20–25sText: "Bayad sa sarili in under a year."
  5. 25–30sCTA: "Book a test ride. Libre. Link sa bio."
Hook C · "Bodega tour"
  1. 0–3sDoor opens on 200+ bikes. "Ito ang pinakamalaking bike bodega sa Marikina."
  2. 3–12sStaff points: kids corner, MTB wall, e-bike row, battery shelf. "Batteries? Meron."
  3. 12–20sReseller loading 10 units into a van. "Wholesale? Meron din."
  4. 20–25sOrigin line: "Started sa sala namin, 2020."
  5. 25–30sCTA: "3 Lilac St., Concepcion Dos. Open daily 9–7."

30-day launch calendar

From sign-off to first test rides in 10 days

Days 1–30
Days 1–3Pixel + Conversions API on the funnel. Instant forms built. Customer exclusion list uploaded.
Days 4–6Bodega shoot: 30 photos, 9 vertical clips, staff on camera. Price tags visible.
Days 7–96 ads built, 3 hook videos edited. Funnel copy finalized with real prices & stock.
Day 10 · LaunchRetail + E-bike campaigns live at ₱500–₱1,000/day. First test rides booked same week.
Days 11–16Daily lead QA: text confirmations within the hour. Kill ads under 1% CTR, scale winners +20%.
Days 17–20Launch Reseller campaign + retargeting layer. Post TikTok hook A/B/C organically.
Days 21–24Spark-boost the top TikTok hook. Add Messenger auto-replies with stock sheet.
Days 25–28Creative refresh: swap 2 lowest ads with customer-UGC versions from first buyers.
Day 29Report: leads, test rides, sales, cost per sale. Compare against projections.
Day 30 · DecideScale to the next tier or hold. Month 2 plan built from real numbers, not benchmarks.
