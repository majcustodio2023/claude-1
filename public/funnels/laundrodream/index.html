Questions

Everything people ask before they book

How much capital do I need?

It depends on the number of machines, your space and whether you add the water station. The consultation gives you an exact figure for your setup; most starter stores fall between a compact 2-washer setup and a full self-service floor.

Is this a franchise? Are there royalties?

No. You own the store and the equipment outright. There are no royalties or monthly franchise fees. LaundroDream supplies the machines, setup, training and support.

I have no experience running a shop.

Most of our owners didn't either. Training covers daily operations, pricing, ticketing, machine care and customer handling. Your staff can be trained with you.

I'm abroad. Can I still open one?

Yes. OFW owners typically run the numbers remotely while a family member or trained manager runs the floor. We handle the location check, installation and training on the ground.

What happens when a machine breaks?

You call the 24/7 emergency repair line. Preventive maintenance is scheduled so breakdowns are rare, and genuine LG parts are stocked.

Is the ₱100,000 profit guaranteed?

No business can guarantee profit. ₱100K is the target scenario at 40 laundry tickets and 100 refills a day with ₱284K monthly expenses. On the call we rebuild it with your actual rent, utilities and market.
