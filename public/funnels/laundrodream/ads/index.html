02
Campaign structure

Three campaigns. One objective: booked consultations.

Campaign 01 · Cold
55%

"Boring business"

Advantage+ audiences seeded with business-owner, franchise and investing interests. NCR, Calabarzon, Central Luzon, Cebu, Davao. Ages 28–55.

  • Optimise for the Schedule event
  • Dynamic creative, 3 angles × 2 formats
  • ₱1,400/day at the recommended tier
Campaign 02 · Retarget
25%

"See the math"

Video viewers 50%+, funnel visitors 30 days, calculator users, form abandoners. Hits them with the ₱100K ledger and the owner stories.

  • Frequency cap 3 per 7 days
  • Calculator screen-record creative
  • Objection ads: "no experience", "I'm abroad"
Campaign 03 · OFW
20%

"Business back home"

Filipinos in UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan. Filipino + English. Lookalike of PH leads.

  • Messenger + instant-form backup
  • Remote-owner angle, family runs the floor
  • Consultation by video call
03
Supporting engines

Organic feeds the machine. Search catches the ready.

Paid social creates demand; two cheaper channels harvest it.

TikTok + Reels organic engine

Three hook videos a week from the shop floor: coin counts, installs, "what ₱384K of laundry looks like." Winners get ₱300/day Spark Ads and feed the retargeting pool.

Google Search safety net

Exact match on "laundry business philippines", "laundromat franchise", "LG commercial washer price", "paano magsimula ng laundry shop". ₱300/day straight to the booking page.

Retargeting layer

Anyone who touches the funnel, the calculator or a video sees the ₱100K ledger and the "who do you call when it breaks" ad for the next 7 days.
