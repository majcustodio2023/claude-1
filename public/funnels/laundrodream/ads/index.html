07
If they don't book right away

Most people need to see you 3 or 4 times first. So we follow up.

Day 1They looked, but didn't bookThey watched half the video, or visited the page, or started the booking form and stopped. Facebook remembers them.
Days 2 to 7They see a reminder adNot the same ad again. The ₱100K math, the "who do you call when it breaks" ad, and a real owner story. At most 3 times a week, so it never feels like spam.
Any dayThey search on GoogleWhen someone types "laundry business Philippines" or "paano magsimula ng laundry shop" into Google, LaundroDream shows up at the top. ₱300 a day covers it.

People who already booked, and current LaundroDream customers, never see these ads.

So about ₱15,000 in ads brings in one ₱750,000 sale. These are average results for Facebook ads in the Philippines. Your real numbers will be higher or lower, and we'll show you the real ones every week.
